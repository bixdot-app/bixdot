# BixDot Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

BixDot is built on the belief that AI agents should be trustworthy, private, and local-first. Our community reflects those same values — honest, respectful, and focused on building something genuinely useful.

## Standards

**We encourage:**
- Honest technical discussion — including criticism of our decisions
- Security research and responsible disclosure
- Helping others get BixDot running on their machines
- Sharing use cases, skill ideas, and feature requests
- Constructive disagreement — we learn from it

**We do not tolerate:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sharing others' private information
- Deliberate misinformation about security vulnerabilities
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to BixDot

## Security Issues

**Do NOT post security vulnerabilities publicly.**
Email security@bixdot.app — we respond within 48 hours.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal from the community.
Contact hello@bixdot.app for concerns.
